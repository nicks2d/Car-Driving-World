Car Driving World 1.0.0 (macOS, Apple Silicon)
==============================================

Install
-------
1. Drag "Car Driving World.app" into your Applications folder.
2. The app is not notarized by Apple. On the first start, right-click the app and choose "Open",
   then "Open" again. If macOS still blocks it: System Settings > Privacy & Security, scroll down
   and click "Open Anyway".

Keys: WASD drive, Space handbrake, Esc menu (settings, all keys), G garage, M map, P photo mode,
Tab camera. All keys are listed in the pause menu under Settings > Controls.

This download contains only car models with a CC BY license (BMW M3 E30, M3 GTS E92, M5 F90,
M4 CSL, Audi S3 in traffic, gas station). Credits: CREDITS.txt and in the game under
Garage > Credits.


Installieren (Deutsch)
----------------------
1. „Car Driving World.app“ in den Ordner „Programme“ ziehen.
2. Die App ist nicht von Apple notarisiert. Beim ersten Start: Rechtsklick auf die App > „Öffnen“,
   dann noch einmal „Öffnen“. Blockiert macOS sie trotzdem: Systemeinstellungen > Datenschutz &
   Sicherheit, nach unten scrollen, „Trotzdem öffnen“.
